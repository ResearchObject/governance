### Machine actionable schemas & Profiles

[RO-Crate-MASP](https://github.com/Language-Research-Technology/ro-crate-masp) with a set of draft profiles and examples under development by Language Data Commons of Australia. 

The [Notes for MASP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17WRkGPIGtoQoSPlTbStBKUyHTzjrOZb620S1gdk0ei8/edit?tab=t.0) was last discussed in December 2025, from where a [draft profile](https://github.com/Language-Research-Technology/ro-crate-masp/blob/main/profiles/ro-crate-masp/profile-crate/profile-documentation.md) was extracted.

This work is to be continued as part of the RO-Crate v2 working group meetings.

### Protocol for adopting packages into the ResearchObject org or otherwise “blessing” them

Marc had drafted [notes for RO-crate software packages](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1sVYizaSNeIbcOeuirRwc3PO0lE_LItudQ4kD7i21gs0/edit?tab=t.0) which were discussed.

Josh suggested a similar approach to Apache Software Foundation's requirement for each project to have its own PMC (Project Management Committee) that manage that repository and makes its decisions. In Zarr they aredDrafting out a _Governance for Zarr Affiliated Software Project_, where afiliated products can have their own funding but have to follow the code of conduct. Consideration of trademark issues is important.  Stian has experience from Apache Incubator for onboarding PMCs.

**Decision*:: Agreement in principle (no quorum) for a  PMC-like governance model for repos under https://github.com/researchobject/ 

**Action**: Eli to communicate to Ro-Crate Rust team that we are positive and are formalising how they can document their governance and be under the RO-Crate banner.

## Any other business

### Using canonical URIs for bioschemas / codemeta
 
Issue [ro-crate#529](https://github.com/ResearchObject/ro-crate/issues/529) is about Bioschemas PIDs having changed. Updating this in RO-Crate JSON-LD context will effectively be a minor update by the [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) rules, meaning we need an RO-Crate 1.3 release.

**Action**: Eli to propose RO-Crate 1.3 plan for next steering committee.
